# ADR-0003 — The errata-and-retraction harvest procedure for `groundtruth/` (S1.1)

- **Date:** 2026-10-13 (Session 9)
- **Status:** accepted — decided by the executor under §2 S1.1, SD12, SD16 and ADR-0001 D2–D4, as
  STATE's NEXT-SINGLE-ACTION (set in Session 6) specified; **five choices are flagged for Al's
  ratification** in *Rationale* (i)–(v), on the ADR-0001 pattern. Until Al rules, the flagged
  choices bind the executor as written here; a contrary ruling amends this file.
- **Scope:** the procedure by which real published proofs with documented errors enter
  `groundtruth/` — where candidates come from, how one is admitted or rejected, what is recorded
  per item, how licences are handled, what artifact form an item takes, and what the S1.4 coverage
  table looks like. **Procedure only: no item is ingested by this ADR.** The informal–formal pairs
  (S1.2), the derivation of canonical artifacts from real items (S1.3), and the ingest tooling in
  `engine/` are their own decisions; this ADR names what it needs from them.

## Context

- **What S1.1 requires (constitution §2):** "Published proofs with documented errors: for each
  item, the original, the erratum or retraction notice, the flaw's location, and its class per
  FLAWS.md (mapping argued in writing; label derivation `documented-by-source`). Licence per SD16."
  SD12: the S1 corpus holds only real proofs with documented status; every item records its source,
  licence, and the document that establishes its status; a real item's ground truth is never
  `by-construction`. §0.3 and SD3 still hold: an item here carries the *source's* documented
  status, never a SYNDEOM verdict.
- **What ADR-0001 already fixes:** the directory layout and `artifact_id` (D2); the storage enum
  `committed` / `regenerable` / `external` (D2); the archival `raw/1` format whose only counts are
  `n_bytes` and `content_hash` and whose location is a transcribed locator (D3); the manifest fields
  an ingest producer must fill — `generator` with `rng: "none"`, `seed: null`, `config.sources`,
  `config.item`, `config.licence`, `config.status {kind, reference}`, `ground_truth.derivation:
  "documented-by-source"`, `statement_status {value, method, reference}`, `proof_status`, `flaws[]`,
  `commanded: {}` (D4); and the derived-canonical-artifact convention: detectors consume only
  `syndeom-informal-md/1` and `syndeom-lean/1`, so every real item that will be scored or placed
  on the proof-length axis gets a derived artifact whose locations are re-expressed and argued in
  the S1.3 record, the `raw/1` parent remaining the item of record (D4).
- **The precedent read first, as STATE required — S-0015 (Barkallah et al., stored PDF, §4.2,
  Appendix D, §7.1):** ArxivMathGradingBench harvests "arXiv math papers that contained errors
  later corrected by their authors". Stage 1 queries the arXiv API for `math.*` papers in a window
  (Appendix D: 2025-01-01 to 2025-07-30; §4.2 says "between 2023 and 2025 inclusive" — the paper
  is inconsistent with itself on the window), skips papers without a revision comment, and keeps
  comments matching two case-insensitive regexes: `correct|errat|error|fix|mistake|bug|flaw|wrong|
  revised|amendment` and `lemma|theorem|proposition`. Stage 2 classifies each comment with an LLM
  (GPT 5.4 nano) as major / minor / none and keeps major and minor. Papers with a non-permissive
  licence or a false-positive comment are removed: 100 candidates became "the 35 papers we use for
  the final dataset". Stage 3 downloads "the PDF for the version before the correction". Each entry
  is (PDF, error location such as "Lemma 1.1"); Table 1 shows comments of the kind "Lemma 4.1 has
  been corrected after N. Tedesco pointed out a mistake in the calculations. The main results are
  unchanged." The labels are **location-only** ("we only have coarse-grained labels of error
  locations, as opposed to details of what the errors are"), and the paper notes that documented
  errors are a subset of the errors present, so measured false positives are upper bounds.
  Three lessons are adopted below, one is rejected (D2: no LLM in admission), and one caveat is
  carried to S4 (Consequences).
- **The routes, checked live this session (Measured, below):** the arXiv API's comment-field
  search, Crossref's update relations (`update-type` filter and `update-to` links), and zbMATH
  Open's title search for errata all answer, with counts. They are discovery routes, not sources:
  a source exists only when an item is retrieved and read (SD11).
- **What the engine has and lacks:** `syndeom-manifest` implements the ADR-0001 types, hashing
  and verdicts with goldens, but no producer writes a ground-truth directory yet. ADR-0001 D4
  requires `generator` to name "the ingest script in `engine/` that wrote the directory", so the
  first harvested item needs that producer first (Consequences).
- **Leakage (SD8):** nothing under `contact/` and nothing AI-generated informs this procedure; the
  6 October release is named only to exclude it (D2 criterion 6).

## Options considered

1. **Candidate sources.** (a) arXiv revision comments only (the S-0015 route); (b) journal errata
   and retractions only (Crossref update relations); (c) both, with zbMATH as the mathematics
   filter for (b) — **chosen (D1)**: (a) gives original and corrected versions as bytes with a
   machine-readable licence, which journal routes rarely do; (b) gives publisher-endorsed notices
   and retractions, which arXiv comments are not; neither alone covers the flaw classes.
2. **Admission.** (a) regex + LLM classification as in S-0015; (b) regex as a candidate finder in
   `lab/` and admission only by the executor reading the notice and both versions, with the class
   argued in writing — **chosen (D2, D3)**: SD10 gives `lab/` no result authority, S1.1 demands an
   argued class, and an LLM's label is neither reproducible (SD6) nor argued.
3. **The label.** (a) location only, as S-0015; (b) location plus FLAWS.md class, severity and
   Lean-catchability argued from the documents — **chosen (D3)**; an item whose class cannot be
   argued is recorded as found-but-unclassifiable, not admitted with a guessed class.
4. **The unit.** (a) the whole paper; (b) the proof of the affected result — **chosen (D6)**: the
   `raw/1` parent is the whole document as received, the derived artifact is the affected result's
   statement and proof, which is what the battery scores and what sits on the length axis.
5. **Harvest order.** (a) curated — pick interesting items; (b) deterministic — a fixed window
   processed in a fixed order with no skipping — **chosen (D8)**: selection by interest is
   selection on the outcome.

## Decision

### D1 — Where candidates come from (three routes; all public documents)

- **Route A — arXiv revision comments.** Papers in `math.*` with at least two versions whose later
  version's comment reports a mathematical correction to a named result. The status document
  (SD12) is the pair *(the correcting version's comment, the corrected version)*; the original is
  the version immediately before the correcting one unless the comment names another. Discovery:
  the arXiv API `search_query` with the `co:` (comment) field and `cat:math.*`, paged
  deterministically (`sortBy=submittedDate`, ascending), then the two S-0015 stage-1 regexes applied
  in `lab/` as a recall-oriented filter. Bytes: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/<id>v<N>` and the source
  `https://arxiv.org/e-print/<id>v<N>`; licence from `https://arxiv.org/abs/<id>v<N>` meta tags
  per version (the machine hazard note in memory: `citation_*` tags; licence can differ by version).
- **Route B — journal errata, corrigenda and retractions via Crossref.** Works with
  `update-type` ∈ {erratum, correction, corrigendum, retraction} whose `update-to` relation names
  the corrected DOI. Discovery: the Crossref REST API `filter=update-type:<type>,type:journal-article`
  with `mailto=` set, restricted to mathematics by Route C (below) or by a recorded ISSN list, paged
  by cursor in a fixed order. The status document is the notice itself; the original is the
  version of record or, when the publisher copy is unreadable, the arXiv version the notice
  corresponds to (then the item is recorded as Route A with the notice as an additional source).
- **Route C — zbMATH Open as the mathematics index.** Documents titled "Erratum …" /
  "Corrigendum …" / "Correction to …" with DOI links, used (i) to confine Route B to mathematics
  and (ii) as a route of its own for notices Crossref does not relate.
- **Excluded by rule:** anything AI-generated (that is S5 — in particular `openai/math` (S-0001),
  its withdrawals (S-0006), and the First Proof batches); secondary indexes whose terms have not
  been read (the Retraction Watch database is not a route until its terms are retrieved and
  recorded); discussions that are not status documents (MathOverflow, blogs, referee reports not
  published by the venue) — they may point to a notice but are never the notice.

### D2 — Admission (every criterion required; the harvest log records the verdict and reason)

1. **A published proof**: an arXiv paper or a journal article containing the proof of a named
   result (lemma, proposition, theorem, corollary).
2. **A public status document** written or endorsed by the authors or the publisher — the
   correcting version's comment plus the corrected version (Route A), or the notice (Routes B, C)
   — that establishes the error's existence. An author's blog post or a third party's note is not
   a status document; a published "comment on …" by a third party is one only if the authors' or
   the venue's response confirms it.
3. **Located**: the status document names the result (and ideally the step) where the error lies;
   "corrected a mistake" without a result name is rejected as unlocated.
4. **Classifiable**: after reading the notice and both versions (the diff is part of the record),
   the executor can argue in writing which FLAWS.md 0.1.0 class the error instantiates (D3). If it
   cannot, the candidate is recorded **found-but-unclassifiable** and is not admitted; the count of
   such candidates per route is reported in S1.4 as part of the coverage statement.
5. **Retrievable**: the original's bytes are retrievable this-session with a recorded licence
   (D5). A notice that cannot be read (paywalled and no accessible copy) leaves the candidate
   **pending**, never admitted on an abstract.
6. **Human-authored**: the proof is the authors' own work as far as the record shows. A paper that
   acknowledges AI assistance in writing, or whose error was found by an AI referee tool, is
   admissible — the proof's authorship is unchanged and the finder is recorded in the item record —
   but a proof the authors state to be AI-generated, and any paper from a known AI-generated
   release, is excluded (S5, SD8).
7. **A mathematical error**: withdrawals for plagiarism, duplicate submission, authorship or
   ethics reasons are not S1 items; a retraction that says only "the main theorem is false" with a
   counterexample is admitted with the location at the statement (D3 maps it) — the error is in
   the proof even when the notice does not say where.

Grey zones go to Al (§4.6) with the candidate held as **escalated**: licence ambiguity (SD16); a
notice whose authorship is unclear; a paper that might be AI-generated.

### D3 — The class mapping, argued in writing per item

- The error *as the status document describes it, confirmed against the diff of the two versions*
  is matched against the FLAWS.md 0.1.0 definitions. The record states the definition clause the
  instance satisfies, the sub-variant where the entry names one, the **severity** from the notice's
  own consequence (main result unchanged and the fix local → `repairable`; a step the fix had to
  re-prove → `gap`; the result withdrawn, weakened or shown false → `fatal`), and
  **`lean_catchable`** from the entry's vocabulary applied to this instance. One error may yield
  several flaw records (a wrong constant that also voided a hypothesis is FLAW-0001 and FLAW-0005);
  each record carries its own location.
- `statement_status` comes from the status document: `true` when the corrected version proves
  the same statement or the notice says the result stands (`method: "source-document"`, the
  reference naming it); `false` when the notice retracts or disproves the statement
  (`method: "counterexample"` when it gives one, else `"source-document"`). **When the status
  document leaves the truth open** ("we do not know whether it is true", S-0015 Table 1), the
  schema has no value to record: such a candidate is held as **pending-truth** and is not ingested
  until Al rules on *Rationale* (ii).
- `proof_status` is `"invalid"` for every S1.1 item by construction of the corpus (the offered
  proof, as published, does not establish the claim); a correct-proof real item belongs to S1.2,
  not here.
- The mapping is written by the executor, naming the model and session, and is the S1.3 record's
  first section; it is a reading of documents, not a verdict on the paper (SD3).

### D4 — The per-item record

Each admitted item is one directory `groundtruth/<artifact_id>/` holding:

- `manifest.json` — `syndeom-manifest/1` per ADR-0001 D4 with `origin: "ground-truth"`,
  `generator` = the ingest producer (D6), `seed: null`, `config.sources` = the S-entries for the
  original and the notice, `config.item` = the arXiv id and version or the DOI of the original,
  `config.licence`, `config.status { "kind": "erratum" | "retraction", "reference": <the notice> }`,
  `ground_truth.derivation: "documented-by-source"`, `statement_status` and `proof_status` per D3,
  `flaws[]` per D3 with `raw/1` locators, `taxonomy_version: "0.1.0"`, `commanded: {}`;
- the original's bytes under their received name (`original.pdf` / `original.tar` for an arXiv
  source bundle) when `storage` is `committed` (D5), else nothing but the hash in the manifest;
- `RECORD.md` — the written record: provenance (route, ids, versions, access times, digests),
  the notice's locating and describing sentences quoted (never more than that from a
  non-redistributable document), the diff summary, the class argument (D3), the licence basis
  (D5), who found the error if stated, and — once S1.3 produces the derived artifact — the
  location re-expression argument. `manifest.notes` names it.

And in the registers: one `S-NNNN` entry per status document and one per original when they are
distinct documents (SOURCES, state VERIFIED or INGESTED per D5), recorded **before** ingestion
(SD16); one line per candidate examined in `groundtruth/HARVEST-LOG.md` (route, id, date, verdict
∈ {admitted, rejected, found-but-unclassifiable, pending, pending-truth, escalated}, reason) so the
admission set is reproducible and no rejection is re-litigated.

### D5 — Licence handling (SD16; ADR-0001 D2)

- Recorded before ingestion, per version, from the primary (the arXiv abstract page's licence
  tag; the publisher's licence statement; Crossref's `license` field as a hint, never as the record).
- A stored copy is `committed` only when the licence permits redistribution of verbatim copies by
  this public repository: CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC BY-NC and CC BY-NC-SA (the S-0005 precedent,
  Session 2: NC permits this program's non-commercial redistribution), CC BY-ND and CC BY-NC-ND
  for the **raw copy only** (verbatim; see Consequences on derived artifacts), public domain, and
  publisher licences that say so in words quoted in the S-entry.
- `external` (digest-only; `config.sources` names the S-entry with URL, access time and SHA-256)
  for arXiv's non-exclusive licence (`http://arxiv.org/licenses/nonexclusive-distrib/1.0/`),
  publisher copyright, and every unstated licence. The hash still pins what was read.
- Ambiguity → escalate to Al, do not ingest (SD16). No notice text is reproduced beyond the
  locating and describing sentences (D4).

### D6 — The artifact form

- **Parent, item of record:** `artifact.kind: "informal"`, `files.informal` = the original as
  received, `format: "raw/1"`, `storage` per D5, `content_hash`, `n_bytes`; every other count
  `null`; each flaw's `location` = `{ "artifact": "informal", "locator": "<text>" }` transcribed
  from the status document (e.g. `"Lemma 4.1, proof, second displayed equation"`). The whole
  document is the parent even though one result is affected: the bytes as received are the
  archival truth (ADR-0001 D3).
- **Derived canonical artifact (S1.3):** one `syndeom-informal-md/1` artifact per item whose
  content is the **affected result's statement (step 0) and its proof (steps 1..n)** as published
  in the original, produced by a versioned deterministic method the S1.3 ADR fixes (from the arXiv
  LaTeX source where available), with `config.derived_from` = the parent's id and each location
  re-expressed as `step`/`span` and argued in `RECORD.md`. Proof length for S1.4 and S3.2 is the
  derived artifact's `n_tokens` / `n_steps`; the parent has no position on the length axis.
- **Producer:** a deterministic ingest producer in `engine/` — working name `syndeom-ingest`,
  `generator.id: "s1_ingest"`, `version: "0.1.0"`, `rng: "none"` — that reads a per-item config
  (the D4 fields) and the stored bytes, computes hashes and counts, writes `manifest.json`, and
  self-checks it against ADR-0001's consistency rules. It does not decide anything: every value it
  writes is an input or a hash. It is built before the first item (Consequences; NEXT).

### D7 — The harvest log and resumability

`groundtruth/HARVEST-LOG.md` is append-only. Its header records each route's query, window,
ordering and the last candidate processed, so a later session resumes exactly where the previous
one stopped. A candidate is examined once; a changed verdict is a new line citing the old one.

### D8 — Window, order, stopping rule (first harvest)

- **Route A window:** `math.*` papers whose correcting version was submitted from 2023-01-01
  through **2026-09-30** — the cut-off precedes the 6 October 2026 release (S-0001) so that the
  arXiv stream cannot overlap the contact era, and the start matches the wider of S-0015's two
  stated windows. **Order:** ascending arXiv identifier of the paper. **No skipping:** every
  candidate the regex filter yields is examined in order and logged; a session stops when its
  budget ends, not when the items stop looking interesting.
- **Routes B and C:** processed after Route A's window is exhausted or when a class has zero Route
  A coverage after the first full pass, in ascending DOI order within each `update-type`.
- **Stopping:** the harvest ends when the window is exhausted; the coverage table (D9) is written
  at Gate S1 over whatever was admitted. No target count is set: a count would be a choice, and
  zero coverage is a finding (S1.4).

### D9 — The S1.4 coverage table and the power statement

One row per FLAW-0001..0014 and a total row; columns: admitted items; items by severity; items by
`lean_catchable` value; `statement_status` true / false; derived-artifact length — count per
log-spaced bin of `n_tokens` (bins fixed at the first S1.4 write from the lengths observed, octaves
from the minimum, and never changed after), with min / median / max `n_steps`; found-but-
unclassifiable candidates attributed to the class the notice suggests, when it suggests one. Below
the table: (1) **zero-coverage classes named** as a finding — S4 cannot validate them; (2) the
**power statement**: for each class, n against N_min once the S3 calibration ADR has computed it
(ADR-0004 D5), and until then the sentence that every real-item cell is expected to be
**underpowered** for the equivalence test and that S4's measurement on them is recall against
documented errors and a false-positive *upper bound* on correct proofs, not a fooling length;
(3) the pending, pending-truth and escalated counts.

## Rationale (the choices flagged for Al)

- **(i) Two files beside ADR-0001's layout:** `RECORD.md` inside each item directory and
  `groundtruth/HARVEST-LOG.md` beside the README. ADR-0001 D2 lists `manifest.json` and the proof
  files only; neither new file is named by the manifest except through `notes`, so this is a
  directory convention, not a schema change. Alternative: a single `PROGRAM/`-level register.
- **(ii) Undetermined statement truth:** ADR-0001 D4 admits only `true` / `false`. Candidates whose
  notice leaves the truth open are held **pending-truth** here. The options are an ADR-0001
  amendment adding `"value": null` with `method: "undetermined"` (then they are ingested with
  `correct: false` derived from `proof_status`), or permanent exclusion. The executor recommends
  the amendment: such items are exactly the triage case §0.2 describes, and excluding them biases
  the corpus toward errors that were easy to repair.
- **(iii) The window and the no-skipping rule (D8):** 2023-01-01 to 2026-09-30, ascending id,
  every candidate logged. Alternative: a narrower window processed completely.
- **(iv) NC-licensed originals committable** on the S-0005 precedent; ND-licensed originals
  committable as verbatim raw copies only.
- **(v) No LLM anywhere in admission (D2):** the regex filter is the only automation, in `lab/`,
  and the executor reads every candidate. Alternative: an LLM pre-classifier as in S-0015, which
  would speed Route A at the cost of SD6 reproducibility of the admission set.

## Consequences

- **STATE:** S1.1 moves from "open — NEXT" to "procedure decided (ADR-0003); no item yet"; ADR-0003
  is no longer reserved; the Gate S1 table gains the harvest log and the producer as evidence
  slots. **NEXT** becomes the ingest producer (D6), because ADR-0001 D4 makes it a prerequisite of
  the first manifest — a change from Session 6's "the first harvested items", with this reason.
- **Engine work before the first item:** `syndeom-ingest` (D6) with a golden test over a fixed
  config and fixed bytes, lint-clean, in the gate. `lab/` gets the Route A candidate finder (the two
  regexes over the API's Atom feed), with zero result authority.
- **S1.3 inherits** the derived-artifact method (LaTeX-source transcription of the affected
  result; determinism and tool pins), the storage of derived artifacts under ND licences (a derived
  transcription is a derivative work: `external` or not produced), and the location re-expression
  argument per item.
- **S4 inherits** the S-0015 caveat: documented errors are a subset of the errors present, so
  recall is measured against the documented set and the false-positive rate on "correct" real
  proofs is an upper bound; S4.1's success criterion is worded accordingly.
- **Cost:** every candidate costs a reading of a notice and a diff; Route A's filtered stream is in
  the thousands. The no-skipping rule means the first harvest sessions will admit few items; that
  is the price of an unselected corpus, and S1.4 says what it bought.
- **Nothing here characterizes any paper** (SD3): an admitted item records what its authors or
  publisher documented, in their words, and the class the executor argued from those words.

# ADR-0004 — Constitution amendment, §2 S3.2: the fooling length is a claim of equivalence to chance, with a design-derived N_min

- **Date:** 2026-10-13 (Session 7)
- **Status:** accepted — **Al's ruling R2 of 2026-10-13, approved as a constitution amendment under
  §3.5 and recorded by the executor in Session 7** (ADR-0000 Addendum 2). Every statistical choice
  below is Al's. The executor contributed the derivation in *Method* and the *Consequences*, and chose
  nothing; where a value is not stated by the ruling it is deferred, not filled.
- **Scope:** the definition of "fooled" and of the **fooling length** in §2 S3.2, and the obligations
  this places on the S3 calibration ADR. No other section of the constitution, no register entry,
  no code, no fixture is touched. Nothing is computed here: no curve, no cell, no N_min number.

## Context

- The ratified S3.2 text (pre-ratification edit 1, ADR-0000 addendum) defined the fooling length by
  "the 95% bootstrap CI of the AUC contains 0.5". That is a *failure to reject* H₀: AUC = 0.5. A wide
  interval satisfies it trivially, so a small or noisy cell is called "fooled" exactly when it says
  least. The N_min floor limited the damage without removing the asymmetry: an interval such as
  [0.49, 0.83] contains 0.5 and still permits substantial discrimination.
- Al's ruling reverses the burden. "Fooled" must be *shown*, by an equivalence test against a
  pre-declared band, and the cell size that makes the test fair is *derived* from the design, not
  picked.
- Provenance: the point was raised in an advisory note of 2026-10-13 that Al relayed (ruling R3,
  file awaited; ADR-0000 Addendum 2). Under SD11 that note is advisory, not evidence, and nothing
  here cites it as rationale. The decision is Al's; the statistics stand on the primary source
  S-0020 and on the derivation in this file.
- Leakage (SD8): no contact-corpus behaviour informed this amendment. No detector exists, no curve
  exists, nothing under `contact/` was opened.

## Options considered

1. **Keep "CI contains 0.5"** (status quo) — rejected: it rewards noise (Context).
2. **Equivalence test with the conventional 90% interval** (two one-sided tests at α = 0.05 each) —
   rejected by Al in favour of the stricter 95% interval.
3. **Equivalence test with a 95% interval inside a pre-declared band, N_min derived from power** —
   **chosen** (D1–D7).
4. **A fixed, round N_min** — rejected: that is a choice, not a derivation ("design-derived, not
   chosen").

## Decision

- **D1 (i) — "Fooled" is a claim of equivalence to chance, not a failure to reject it.** Every
  reported cell carries exactly one of three labels: **fooled** (criterion D3 met, N ≥ N_min);
  **not distinguished from chance** (the CI contains 0.5 but is not inside the band, N ≥ N_min);
  **underpowered** (N < N_min, whatever the interval shows). A cell whose AUC confidence interval
  merely contains 0.5 is never reported as fooled.
- **D2 (ii) — Equivalence band:** ±0.05 around AUC 0.5, i.e. **[0.45, 0.55]**; δ = 0.05.
- **D3 (iii) — Criterion:** the **95% bootstrap confidence interval of the AUC lies entirely inside
  the band.** This is two one-sided tests (TOST) at **α = 0.025 each** — stricter than the
  conventional 90%-interval TOST (α = 0.05 per side), and this ADR says so explicitly. The
  convention is stated on Al's authority; no citation for it is entered this session (SD11).
- **D4 (iv) — Fooling length,** per detector per flaw class: the smallest proof length L at which a
  cell with N ≥ N_min satisfies D3. Proof length remains swept log-spaced over the regimes the S1
  corpus exhibits (unchanged from the ratified text).
- **D5 (v) — N_min is design-derived, not chosen:** the smallest *balanced* cell (n flawed versus
  n matched correct) at which the test in D3 has **90% power to declare equivalence when the true
  AUC is exactly 0.5.** The derivation method is fixed in *Method* below — from the null-hypothesis
  variance of the Mann–Whitney U statistic, primary source **S-0020** (retrieved and read this
  session; eq. (5), p. 55). **The computed number is deferred to the S3 calibration ADR,** which
  computes it *before any curve exists* and **confirms it by simulation against the bootstrap
  procedure actually used.**
- **D6 (vi) — Dependence:** items derived from one seed theorem are not independent. The S3
  calibration ADR states **how effective N is computed** — for example a cluster bootstrap by seed
  theorem — *before any curve is computed*. N in D4 and D5 means effective N.
- **D7 (vii) — Consequences, stated as decided:** cells too expensive to fill to N_min —
  human-referee cells under SD13, detectors that need a per-item Lean build across the full grid —
  are reported as **underpowered**. That is the intended behaviour, not a defect.

## Method (fixed here; the number is computed in the S3 calibration ADR)

**Notation.** A cell holds n flawed items with detector scores x₁ … xₙ and m matched-correct items
with scores y₁ … yₘ (balanced: m = n). The detector's AUC is the probability that it scores a flawed
item above a matched-correct one; its empirical estimate is

  Â = U / (nm),  U = #{(i, j) : xᵢ > yⱼ} (+ ½ for each tie),

the Mann–Whitney count, because the area under the empirical ROC is by construction the fraction of
(flawed, correct) pairs the detector ranks in the right order. Under the null hypothesis H₀ that the
two score distributions are identical and continuous (no ties), **S-0020** gives, for U counted
either way round:

  E(U) = nm / 2  (S-0020 eq. (2), p. 51),  Var(U) = E[(U − nm/2)²] = nm (n + m + 1) / 12  (S-0020 eq. (5), p. 55).

Hence Var(Â) = (n + m + 1) / (12 nm); balanced, **Var₀(Â) = (2n + 1) / (12 n²)** and
SE₀(n) = √((2n + 1) / (12 n²)).

**Independent check of the variance** (so that the method does not rest on the retrieval alone).
Write Â = (1/nm) Σᵢ Σⱼ Iᵢⱼ with Iᵢⱼ = 1[xᵢ > yⱼ]. Under H₀ each Iᵢⱼ has mean ½ and variance ¼. Two
indicators sharing one index — Iᵢⱼ and Iᵢₖ, j ≠ k — are both 1 exactly when xᵢ is the largest of
three exchangeable continuous variables, probability ⅓, so their covariance is ⅓ − ¼ = 1/12; the
same for Iᵢⱼ and Iₗⱼ, i ≠ l; indicators sharing no index are independent. Ordered pairs sharing
exactly the x-index number nm(m − 1), those sharing exactly the y-index nm(n − 1). Therefore
Var(Σ Iᵢⱼ) = nm/4 + nm(m − 1)/12 + nm(n − 1)/12 = nm(n + m + 1)/12, which is eq. (5).

**Power of the test at the centre of the band** (normal approximation, the analytic starting value).
Let z_{1−α} denote the standard-normal upper quantile, α = 0.025 as in D3, and β = 0.10 for 90%
power. The 95% interval is Â ± z_{0.975}·SE; it lies inside [0.5 − δ, 0.5 + δ] exactly when
|Â − 0.5| < δ − z_{0.975}·SE. When the true AUC is 0.5, (Â − 0.5)/SE₀ is approximately standard
normal, so

  power(n) = 2 Φ(δ / SE₀(n) − z_{0.975}) − 1,  and power(n) = 0.90 ⇔ SE₀(n) = δ / (z_{0.975} + z_{0.95}).

**What the S3 calibration ADR does with this, in order, before any curve:**

1. Solves (2n + 1) / (12 n²) = (δ / (z_{0.975} + z_{0.95}))² with δ = 0.05 for the smallest integer
   n — the analytic starting value, recorded with the quantiles used.
2. Confirms by simulation against the bootstrap procedure actually used: generates balanced cells of
   size n under H₀ with the tie structure of the actual detectors (binary and coarse detectors are
   heavily tied, and the no-ties variance above does not describe them), runs the real bootstrap
   interval on each, and records the fraction declared equivalent; raises n until that fraction is
   ≥ 0.90. The confirmed n is N_min.
3. Records, at the same n, the declaration rate when the true AUC is 0.45 and 0.55 (the equivalence
   test's type-I error at the band edges, which D3 fixes at 0.025 per side) and the bootstrap
   interval's coverage at AUC 0.5 (the TOST's α relies on it).
4. States the effective-N rule of D6 and runs steps 2–3 at the cluster level the rule defines, so that
   N_min counts what the bootstrap actually resamples.

No step is performed in this ADR. The S3 calibration ADR's number is the one of record; this ADR
owns only the method.

## Consequences

- §2 S3.2 is replaced in the same commit by text matching D1–D7, citing this ADR; an *Amendments*
  line is added to the constitution's header and footer. The constitution's version string ("v1.0")
  is unchanged: no rule assigns version numbers to amendments, and inventing one would be an
  executor choice (SD7). If Al wants a versioning rule, it is a ruling.
- STATE: ADR-0003 remains reserved for S1.1; **ADR-0005 is the next free number**; the S3 calibration
  ADR's obligations (D5 steps 1–4, D6) are listed under standing rulings so a cold successor finds
  them without reading this file first.
- Reporting vocabulary from S3 onward: *fooled* / *not distinguished from chance* / *underpowered*,
  always with the band, the interval, N (effective) and L. "Fooled" never appears without them.
- §0.5(b), the pre-declared negative result ("no detector in the battery beats chance"), is not
  amended by this ADR. `analysis/README.md`'s mention of "the S3 calibration ADR's N_min declaration"
  stands and now means: computed by this method and confirmed by simulation. CLAIM-0006's scoped
  novelty statement ("a power-floored fooling length (§2 S3.2)") remains true; no register entry
  changes.
- Cost: cells are larger than under the old definition, and some cannot be filled. Human-referee
  cells (SD13) and detectors needing a per-item Lean build across the full grid are expected to be
  reported underpowered (D7). That is a finding the program pre-declares, not a defect to engineer
  around; it is also why D6 matters — inflating N with dependent variants of one seed theorem would
  manufacture power.
- Sources: S-0020 VERIFIED this session (archived publisher PDF read; digest-only, not committed —
  licence not stated on the copy). The method's two uses of it are eqs. (2) and (5); the independent
  derivation above makes the method self-contained if the citation were ever withdrawn.

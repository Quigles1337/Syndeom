# A square plus one is at least twice the number

**Theorem.** For every real number $x$, $x^2 + 1 \ge 2x$.

**Proof.**

**Step 1.** Expanding the square, $(x - 1)^2 = x^2 - 2x - 1$.

**Step 2.** A square is non-negative, so $x^2 - 2x + 1 = (x - 1)^2 \ge 0$.

**Step 3.** Adding $2x$ to both sides gives $x^2 + 1 \ge 2x$.

∎

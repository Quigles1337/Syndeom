# A polynomial identity in two integer variables

**Theorem.** For all integers $x$ and $y$, $(x - 2y)^2 = x^2 - 4xy + 4y^2$.

**Proof.**

**Step 1.** Lemma 1. For all integers $x$ and $y$, $(-4yx) + (x^2 + 4 \cdot y \cdot y) = (4 \cdot y \cdot y) + (-4yx + x^2)$; both sides expand to the same polynomial.

**Step 2.** Lemma 2. For all integers $x$ and $y$, $x(x - 4y) + 4y^2 = (4 \cdot y \cdot y) + (-4xy + x \cdot x)$; both sides expand to the same polynomial.

**Step 3.** Expanding the square and factoring out $x$, $(x - 2y)^2 = 4 \cdot y \cdot y + x(x - 4y)$.

**Step 4.** Rearranging the terms, $4 \cdot y \cdot y + x(x - 4y) = 4y^2 - 4yx + x \cdot x$.

**Step 5.** Rearranging the terms, $4y^2 - 4yx + x \cdot x = x^2 - 4yx + 4 \cdot y \cdot y$.

**Step 6.** Rearranging the terms, $x^2 - 4yx + 4 \cdot y \cdot y = x^2 - 4yx + 4y^2$.

**Step 7.** Regrouping the terms, $x^2 - 4yx + 4y^2 = (-4xy + x^2) + (4 \cdot y \cdot y)$.

**Step 8.** Rearranging the terms, $(-4xy + x^2) + (4 \cdot y \cdot y) = x^2 + 4 \cdot y \cdot y - 4yx$.

**Step 9.** Factoring out $y$, $x^2 + 4 \cdot y \cdot y - 4yx = y(-4x + 4y) + x \cdot x$.

**Step 10.** Regrouping the terms, $y(-4x + 4y) + x \cdot x = (-4yx) + (x^2 + 4 \cdot y \cdot y)$.

**Step 11.** By Lemma 1, $(-4yx) + (x^2 + 4 \cdot y \cdot y) = (4 \cdot y \cdot y) + (-4yx + x^2)$.

**Step 12.** Rearranging the terms, $(4 \cdot y \cdot y) + (-4yx + x^2) = -4xy + 4 \cdot y \cdot y + x^2$.

**Step 13.** Regrouping the terms, $-4xy + 4 \cdot y \cdot y + x^2 = (-4yx) + (x^2 + 4y^2)$.

**Step 14.** Rearranging the terms, $(-4yx) + (x^2 + 4y^2) = x \cdot x - 4yx + 4 \cdot y \cdot y$.

**Step 15.** Factoring out $y$, $x \cdot x - 4yx + 4 \cdot y \cdot y = y(-4x + 4y) + x \cdot x$.

**Step 16.** Rearranging the terms, $y(-4x + 4y) + x \cdot x = 4y^2 - 4xy + x \cdot x$.

**Step 17.** Rearranging the terms, $4y^2 - 4xy + x \cdot x = 4 \cdot y \cdot y + x \cdot x - 4yx$.

**Step 18.** Rearranging the terms, $4 \cdot y \cdot y + x \cdot x - 4yx = -4xy + x \cdot x + 4y^2$.

**Step 19.** Rearranging the terms, $-4xy + x \cdot x + 4y^2 = -4yx + x^2 + 4 \cdot y \cdot y$.

**Step 20.** Factoring out $x$, $-4yx + x^2 + 4 \cdot y \cdot y = x(x - 4y) + 4y^2$.

**Step 21.** Factoring out $y$, $x(x - 4y) + 4y^2 = x \cdot x + y(-4x + 4y)$.

**Step 22.** Regrouping the terms, $x \cdot x + y(-4x + 4y) = (-4yx + 4y^2) + (x^2)$.

**Step 23.** Regrouping the terms, $(-4yx + 4y^2) + (x^2) = (-4xy) + (4y^2 + x \cdot x)$.

**Step 24.** Factoring out $x$, $(-4xy) + (4y^2 + x \cdot x) = x(x - 4y) + 4y^2$.

**Step 25.** By Lemma 2, $x(x - 4y) + 4y^2 = (4 \cdot y \cdot y) + (-4xy + x \cdot x)$.

**Step 26.** Rearranging the terms, $(4 \cdot y \cdot y) + (-4xy + x \cdot x) = x^2 - 4xy + 4y^2$.

∎
